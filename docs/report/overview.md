## Overview

To track the incomes and expenses, you need to assign a category to
your transactions[^1].

::: important
Reports are valued in your currency (and not in ZEC). The currency
is chosen in the Settings page; it is USD by default.
:::

Therefore you also need to have the price of 1 ZEC at the time of
the transaction.

When you make a payment, the price shown in the "Send" form is recorded with
the transaction. When you receive ZEC, the transaction has no price ("N/A")
until you fetch it.

## Fetching Tx Prices

For convenience, the market prices can be retrieved from CoinGecko for the
past year[^2]. Choose "Fetch Tx Prices" in the account page menu: every
transaction of the account that has no price gets the market price of its
time.

[^1]: Transactions that don't have a category are excluded from the reports.
[^2]: CoinGecko has older data but getting more than the past year requires a
    commercial license.
