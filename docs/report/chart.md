title: Charts
---

Zkool currently has two report charts[^1]. Open them with the "Charts" option
of the account page menu. Both of them can be limited to a given time period
with the "From" and "To" dates at the top of the page.

::: important
Due to some technical issue, **charts are not available on Linux**.
:::

## Income/Expense By Category

This is a pie chart that shows your total of income or expenses by category.

- Switch between Income and Spending with the dropdown.

![Income =450x](./images/01.report.png)

## Category by Time

Given a category, this chart plots your spending (or income) on a time axis. If
the cumulative box is checked, it shows how the total amount in the given
category grows over time.

![Category =450x](./images/02.food.png)

[^1]: More charts will be added later.
