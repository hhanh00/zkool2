title: Charts
---

Zkool currently has two report charts[^1]. Open them with the "Charts" option
of the account page menu. Both of them can be limited to a given time period
with the "From" and "To" dates at the top of the page.

## Income/Expense By Category

This is a donut chart that shows your total of income or expenses by category,
with a legend of the amounts and their share. Tap on a slice, or on its legend
entry, to bring it forward.

- Switch between Income and Spending with the dropdown.

![Income =450x](./images/01.report.png)

The eight largest categories get a slice each; smaller ones are summed into
"Other".

## Category by Time

Given a category, this chart plots your spending (or income) on a time axis,
one point per transaction. Touch a point to see its date and amount. If the
cumulative box is checked, it shows how the total amount in the given category
grows over time.

![Category =450x](./images/13.category.png)

[^1]: More charts will be added later.
