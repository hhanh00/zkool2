---
title: Since 6.37
---

The 6.38 release makes synchronization about three times faster over a
direct lightwalletd connection, repairs how a chain reorganization is
handled, and stops a silent server from hanging the app.

*Released 24 November 2026 as [6.38.0](https://github.com/hhanh00/zkool2/releases/tag/zkool-v6.38.0).*

## Faster synchronization

Four changes shorten a sync. The first two apply to a lightwalletd server
reached directly: Tor, Nym, proxy and Zebra connections keep their single
block stream.

- **Blocks arrive over several connections.** A long range is downloaded as
  consecutive slices on six streams, then put back in height order for the
  scan. Until now the scan spent more time waiting for blocks than scanning
  them.
- **The download goes to the fastest backends.** A server such as zec.rocks
  sits behind a front end that spreads requests over backends in several
  regions, which differ by four to seven times in speed. ZKool now measures
  each backend and steers its connections to the best ones. A server that
  does not name its backends is not steered.
- **Finished parts of the note commitment trees are no longer hashed.** When
  a completed subtree holds none of your unspent notes, ZKool takes its root
  from the server instead of hashing every note in it. Every 16th subtree is
  still hashed and compared with the server's root, and a mismatch fails the
  sync. Zebra does not serve these roots, so a Full Node sync hashes
  everything as before.

- **Orchard and Ironwood notes are tried in batches.** Finding your notes
  means trying to decrypt every shielded output of every block with your
  viewing key. ZKool used to do the key agreement behind each attempt one
  output at a time; it now computes them together, a chunk of outputs at a
  time, with the zakura-udon library. On 200,000 recent Mainnet blocks and 4
  cores, this step went from 9.8 to 3.1 seconds for Orchard and from 33.7 to
  9.6 seconds for Ironwood, and the whole scan from 47.9 to 17.1 seconds.
  Sapling is unchanged.

On a 1.3 million block Mainnet range with an empty wallet, a sync that took
201 to 233 seconds in 6.37 now takes 58 to 63 seconds.

The "Actions per Sync" setting is gone. ZKool now saves its progress every
15 seconds of download, so a cancelled or interrupted sync loses at most
that much. Cancelling also takes effect sooner: it no longer waits for
blocks already downloaded to be scanned, nor for a server that does not
answer.

## Chain reorganizations

- A reorganization used to rewind an account to its latest witness, which
  sent an account without notes back to its birth height, and could leave a
  range of blocks unscanned. Each account now rewinds to the latest height
  below the reorganization where all its unspent notes have a witness, and
  the sync plans again from there. A manual rewind follows the same rule.
- A wallet left stuck by the old behavior repairs itself at the start of the
  next sync.
- A reorganization under an account with only transparent addresses is now
  detected. It went unnoticed before, and transactions from orphaned blocks
  stayed in the history.
- A mismatch at a block more than 100 deep fails the sync and rewinds
  nothing.

## A silent server no longer hangs the app

A lightwalletd server that accepted a request and never answered left the
sync waiting forever, with every later sync queued behind it. Every call now
fails once the server has sent nothing for a set time: 60 seconds during a
sync over a direct connection, 2 minutes over Tor, Nym or a proxy. A server
that keeps sending is never cut off. Connecting is bounded too, at 15
seconds direct and 2 minutes over the slow transports, and calls to a Zebra
node have the same limits.

The mempool monitor reopens its stream when it goes quiet instead of
stopping, which happened on Testnet and Regtest when a block took long to
come.

## New account seed phrase

The dialog that shows the seed phrase of a new account opens with every
word masked. Tick **Show seed phrase** to reveal it, and untick it to hide
it again. The phrase can only be copied while it is shown. See
[Accounts](../guide/accounts.md).

## ZKool

The app is named ZKool on every platform. Existing wallets keep opening: the
default database name is still `zkool`. The Flatpak app ID is now
`cc.methyl.zkool` and replaces `cc.methyl.Zkool`.

The app icon has padding around it and no longer carries the ZKOOL wordmark,
since the name already appears next to it. The splash logo no longer
overflows the screen on iOS and older Android versions, and is no longer
cropped on Android 12 and later.

## Also

- The account list highlights the current account. Deleting it from the
  list leaves you on the list, instead of on the page of an account that no
  longer exists, and the list no longer blanks on each delete.
- The popups that announced the start and the end of a sync are removed, as
  is the "Height" label of the sync bar.
- Sending no longer shows a toast with the transaction hex, and the other
  send toasts are reworded.
- The gradient of the balance panel fills the row when the balance is 0.
- On the Key Tree page, a long key no longer overflows its row, and an
  indented row stays inside the list.
- GraphQL: `decodePczt` returns the `txid` of the prepared transaction, so a
  client can record a payment's txid before it signs and broadcasts. The
  field is null when the transaction's effects are not final or it predates
  v5.
