The 6.33 and 6.34 releases came out the same day. Together they bring swaps to the wallet and change how new accounts handle change.

*Released 11 October 2026 as 6.33.0 and 6.34.0.*

## Swaps

An account's menu has a new Swaps entry. From there you can swap ZEC for USDT, USDC, SOL or ETH on Solana, Ethereum, Base or Polygon, or the other way round, receiving ZEC for one of those assets. The swaps are carried by NEAR Intents.

The form takes the direction, the asset and its network, the amount on whichever side you want fixed, the receiving or refund address, and a slippage tolerance. The next page shows the quote, with the estimated costs and the minimum you receive, before you confirm. A ZEC to asset swap is funded through the usual transaction flow, so the transaction plan and the fee are the ones you know. An asset to ZEC swap gives you a deposit address to pay from the other wallet.

The Swaps page lists the account's swaps with their status (waiting for deposit, processing, completed, refunded, failed or expired), refreshes them on demand, and can hide the closed ones or delete them. A swap that is never funded expires after a one day grace period.

Swaps are only available in builds made with a NEAR Intents API key, which the official releases are. A build without one says so instead of failing later.

## Internal change by default

New and recovered accounts now send their change to internal addresses, the way the Zcash protocol intends. The setting can be changed on the account edit form, and so can the birth height. Leaving the form with unsaved edits asks whether to save or discard them, and the form warns before an edit that resets the synchronization data. See the [Edit an Account](../recipe/account.md) recipe.

## Also

- The wallet is built on the published NU7 libraries while still following the protocol deployed on the network.
- Cancelling a synchronization is no longer reported as an error.
