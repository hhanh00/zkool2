Better control over the notes, and the Orchard migration in one shot.

*Released 31 July 2026, last patch 6.26.1 on 2 August 2026.*

## Notes

The notes page can group the notes by pool, hide the dust (ZEC notes of 5000 zats or less), lock or unlock all the notes of a pool from its header, and invert the lock state of every note in one tap. See the [Locking Notes](../recipe/coin.md) recipe.

![The notes page =450x](../recipe/images/30.coin.png)

## Orchard migration

The migration from Orchard to Ironwood can run as a single operation. It streams its progress, skips a step when no block has arrived since the last broadcast, and can be cancelled. Leaving the page during a migration asks for confirmation. Migration transactions are labelled as such in the history.

## 6.26.1

- Ironwood transactions are detected in the mempool.
- The mempool keys are refreshed after each new block.
