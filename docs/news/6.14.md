The Cloud Vault, signed multisig messages and synchronization fixes.

*Released 18 April 2026 as 6.14.0, last patch 6.14.6 on 1 May 2026.*

## Cloud Vault

The Cloud Vault keeps an encrypted copy of your account keys on Google Drive. It is protected by a master password, and a passkey can unlock it on the platforms that support them. Recovering restores the accounts from the master password alone. The vault is lazy: it syncs when something changed, and it skips accounts restored from a short seed phrase. The option is behind expert mode in Settings, and Android's automatic app backup is turned off so that the only copy of your keys is the one you chose.

The six patch releases were the vault's shakedown: passkeys on Android and iOS, Google sign-in on iOS, stale passkeys re-registered, and confirmation messages at each step.

## Multisig messages

A key generation starts with a round 0 in which each participant publishes an ed25519 key. The messages of the following rounds are signed with it and verified on receipt.

## Also

- A missing witness is detected and a resynchronization is offered instead of a failed payment.
- The synchronization no longer sends an extra chunk of blocks after a reorg or an abort, and the account page no longer flashes when it ends.
- The transparent address at index 0 is always created.
- The pool selection of a unified address is fixed.
- Transaction details are fetched from the account manager too.
