title: Since 6.21
---

Small improvements to the history, the logs and the notifications.

*Released 26 June 2026.*

## Also

- The history search also matches the transaction id.
- The verbose synchronization, database and memo logs only appear in expert mode, and wallet data no longer appears in the logs of release builds.
- A successful send shows a dismiss button.
- Notifications are shown below the app bar instead of over it.
- The viewing keys page centres the QR code and drops the fingerprint.
- Amounts of shielded assets are shown in the asset's units rather than as ZEC.
- The synchronization check runs before the transaction plan is computed, so the plan you confirm is never stale.
- Autosync restarts when its interval is set back to a positive value.
- The default block explorer URL is updated.
