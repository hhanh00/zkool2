A table view for the history, your own memos on transactions, and a synchronization check before sending.

*Released 13 June 2026.*

## History table

The transaction history can be shown as a table with the type, date, amount, amount in your currency, price, transaction id, height, category, memo and asset of each transaction. Switch between the list and the table from the account page menu. See the [Account](../guide/account.md) guide.

## Your own memos

Long press a memo to replace it with your own text. The original memo from the chain is kept and comes back when you clear yours. User memos are shown in orange, in the history, the transaction details and the Memos tab.

## Synchronization before sending

If the account is behind the chain when you send, Zkool offers to synchronize first or to send anyway. After importing or restoring an account, it offers to synchronize it right away.
