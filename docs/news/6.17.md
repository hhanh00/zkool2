Pick your server and your proxy, choose a theme, and manage shielded assets from a tab.

*Released 8 June 2026 as 6.17.0, last patch 6.17.7 on 9 June 2026.*

## Servers and proxies

The lightwalletd server can be chosen from a list, with its URL scheme, and an onion address turns Tor on by itself. Connections can go through an external proxy, SOCKS5 or HTTP, or through the embedded Arti Tor client; the proxy address and the Tor toggle are in Settings. See [Using TOR](../recipe/tor.md).

## Themes

A theme selector, on its own settings page, changes the colours of the app and applies them at once.

## Shielded assets

The ZSA holdings move from the account menu to a tab of the account page, where an asset can be renamed in place and the holdings exported. Tapping a holding opens the issuance form prefilled to issue more of that asset.

## Also

- On the send page, `@` followed by an account name resolves to that account's address, with the account's avatar in the suggestions.
- The MAX button turns on "receiver pays fees", so the whole balance can be sent.
- The Send button is disabled while the transaction is broadcast.
- Edit Account is in the account page menu, and the viewing keys are under Backup.
- Turning Pin Lock or the Cloud Vault on or off asks for authentication, and devices without biometric hardware skip the biometric prompt.
- Account rows in the manager can be tapped anywhere, which fixes opening an account on desktop.
- The app leaves room for the system navigation bar on edge to edge screens.
- The GitHub release notes list the changes of the whole minor series.
